Brisbane Braces for Severe Weather as Storm Front Approaches

By Sarah Mitchell, Weather Correspondent

Residents across Brisbane and the Indooroopilly area are being urged to prepare for a significant weather event tonight, with the Bureau of Meteorology forecasting persistent heavy showers and potential flash flooding.

A severe weather band moving through south-east Queensland is expected to bring a 90 per cent chance of continued rainfall throughout the evening, with heavy falls anticipated at times. Meteorologists warn that the system could produce localised flooding in low-lying areas.

"We're seeing a very active weather pattern developing," said a Bureau spokesperson. "Temperatures will remain humid, sitting between 18 and 20 degrees, with light northern and north-eastern breezes doing little to ease conditions."

The rainfall is predicted to persist into Tuesday morning before the system moves offshore, bringing drier weather to the region later in the week. Residents are advised to carry umbrellas and exercise caution on the roads, particularly around known flood-prone intersections.

Local councils have activated emergency response protocols as a precaution. The State Emergency Service has reported an increase in call-outs over the past 24 hours, primarily related to minor property damage and fallen tree branches.

Commuters are encouraged to allow extra travel time and to check road conditions before setting out. Public transport services are operating normally but delays are possible if conditions deteriorate further.
